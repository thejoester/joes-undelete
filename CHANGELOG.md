# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-26
### Added
- Initial release.
- **Recycle bin:** deleted documents are kept so you can bring them back, including things like items on an actor or tokens in a scene. GMs and players each have their own bin.
- **Restore:** recreate a deleted document where it was. If its parent was deleted too and is still in the bin, you can restore that as well.
- **Preview:** take a read-only look at a deleted document before restoring it.
- **Search and cleanup:** search by type, name or parent, permanently delete single entries, or empty the whole bin.
- **History limits:** settings for how many entries are held during a session and how many are saved between reloads. Entries that won't survive a reload are marked.
- **Easy access:** open the bin from the Settings sidebar or with a macro.
